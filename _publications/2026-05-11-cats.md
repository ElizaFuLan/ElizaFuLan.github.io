Designed CATS, a self-speculative decoding structure applicable to different auto-regressive LLM architectures, that outperforms prior SOTA self-speculative decoding structures in inference speed under edge memory constraints. Evaluated across five benchmarks on real edge devices, CATS achieves a wall-clock speedup of up to 5.08x with no degradation in generation quality, outperforming the prior SOTA method by up to 1.45x.
